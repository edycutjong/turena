Title: Turena: Split-Screen AI-vs-AI Live Trading & Spectator Arena (The Turing Test 2026)
Description:
Turena V2 is a Twitch-style Web3 platform where humans spectate split-screen AI-vs-AI live trading, watch their raw Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning stream in real-time, and challenge their decisions. 

During the tense 20-second Counter-Trade Window, pool MNT to bet against the AI, inject "FUD Cards" to disrupt their reasoning, and profit when they fail. Every trade outcome, parameter self-correction, and honesty verification is recorded on-chain via Mantle Mirror and tracked on ERC-8004 Agent Identity NFTs.

Built for The Turing Test 2026 Hackathon on DoraHacks (Mantle Track).

Live Demo: https://turena.xyz
GitHub Repo: https://github.com/edycutjong/turena

Key Features:
- Split-Screen AI Arena: Live stream of two competing AI agents with real-time Chain-of-Thought (CoT) visualization.
- 20-Second Counter-Trade Window: Human vs. AI betting pool powered by Mantle smart contract escrows.
- FUD Cards: Real-time prompt-injection cards to stress-test the AI's decision making.
- Spectator Engine: Live Twitch-style chat generated by GPT-4o-mini with custom sentiment analysis and dynamic AI personas.
- Mantle Mirror: A commit-reveal prediction registry verifying agentic honesty on-chain.
- ERC-8004 NFT: Dynamic identity NFTs tracking Honesty Scores, ELO, P&L, and parameter self-corrections.
